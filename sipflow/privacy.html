SipFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

SipFlow is a water tracker for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: SipFlow does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your buddy's name, the answers you give during setup (such as sex, age group and weight, used only to estimate your sip size and daily goal), your settings, sip history, streak, levels and outfits are stored only on your device. On iPhone the app, its widgets and the Apple Watch app share this data with each other on your devices. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't do

SipFlow has no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Purchases

SipFlow Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process your payment under their own privacy policies. SipFlow only asks the store whether Plus is active on your account; it never receives your name, email or payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Their text includes your buddy's name and never leaves the device.

Sharing

When you tap Share on the streak card, SipFlow creates a picture and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes. SipFlow doesn't send it anywhere by itself.

Backups

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup, or Android backup) may include SipFlow's data according to your device settings. That backup is handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete all data in SipFlow, or delete the app. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

SipFlow is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children. For younger users, setup asks an adult to confirm the daily goal.

Health

SipFlow estimates how much you drink from your taps. It does not measure intake and is not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
