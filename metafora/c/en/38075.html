Grounding kit

  • PTSD
  • Presence
  • Body awareness
  • Self-soothing

A kit for clients who get overwhelmed by flashbacks or dissociation: a small pouch or box of 5–7 items, put together in session. Through taste, smell, touch or sight, each item sends a strong but safe “I am here and now” signal. The aim is not to comfort, as in the card “Self-soothing through the senses,” but to wake attention up to the present.

What to include: extra-strong mints or gum, sour candies, a pebble or a spiky massage ball, a small bottle with a scent (citrus, coffee), a photo from present-day life, a card with orientation phrases: “My name is…, I am … years old, the year is …, I am in ….”

How to run

  1. 1.Try out each item in session while calm: does it bring attention back to the present?
  1. 2.Be sure to check that no smell, taste or texture is linked to what happened:
  • ✓even a pleasant perfume can turn out to be a reminder.
  1. 3.Decide where the kit will be kept, and make a pocket-size mini version.
  1. 4.Practice: on noticing the first signs of a flashback or “switching off,” the client takes one item, describes it aloud in detail (color, temperature, taste), then looks around the room.
  1. 5.After a real episode, the client writes down what worked.

Caution: no painful stimuli, such as holding ice in the hand for a long time or pinching oneself: bringing attention back through pain is not advisable. Take allergies into account. A teen can put the kit together with a parent or another trusted adult, if they want to. The kit supports therapy; it does not replace it.
