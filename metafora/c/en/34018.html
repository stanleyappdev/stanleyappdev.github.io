Game

Four fairy-tale languages

  • Imagination
  • Team building
  • Communication
  • Attention and memory

Goal: in a game, feel what it is like not to be understood, and practice ways of understanding each other: gestures, asking again, patience.

Approximate time: 25-35 minutes.

Participants: 8-24 in four teams.

Materials: four cards with language rules, paper and pencils for hints.

  1. 1.Hand out the languages

Each team gets a card with the rule of its language; the team makes up a name for the language itself.

  • ✓Add “-ko” after every word: “hello-ko, friend-ko”.
  • ✓Say every word very slowly and in a sing-song voice.
  • ✓Repeat the first syllable of every word: “wea-weather”, “su-sunny”.
  • ✓Speak only in two-word sentences: “Nice weather”, “Let’s play”.
  1. 2.Practice

The teams practice among themselves for 3-5 minutes.

  1. 3.Meetings

Participants from different teams form pairs or threes and try to talk about a simple topic: say hello, ask about the weather, invite someone to play. After 3 minutes change partners. Cards can be held in front of you, and gestures and drawings used as hints.

  1. 4.Coming back

After the game everyone says something together in ordinary language to step out of the role.

Discussion

  • ✓What helped you understand the other person?
  • ✓How did you feel when you weren’t understood?
  • ✓What can you do when you are with someone who finds it hard to understand you?

Closing

Each team names one way of understanding each other that worked best.

If someone mocks

Remind everyone: we are playing by made-up rules and we don’t imitate real accents or speech differences. If someone does, stop the game and explain the difference.
