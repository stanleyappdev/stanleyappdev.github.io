Exercise

Metaphor cards: a collage of my support

  • Resources
  • Inner support
  • Support
  • Self-care

The collage gathers on one sheet what helps the client keep going and becomes a reminder for a hard day. 30–40 minutes.

Caution: a card may bring to mind a person or a place that is out of reach right now (family far away, someone who is gone, a home left behind in another city). Make room for the client to talk about it; what goes on the sheet is the client’s decision.

What you need: an imagery or scene deck, 20–30 cards face up (for a child, 8–12 cards from a children’s deck); an A3 sheet, markers, magazines, scissors, glue.

How to run

  1. 1.The client chooses four cards face up: “A person who supports me,” “A place where I feel good,” “An activity that restores my strength,” “A quality of mine I can rely on.” For each one: “What is happening here? Whom or what is it about?”
  1. 2.The client places the cards in the center of the sheet and, around them, uses drawings, words, and clippings to show what each one evokes.
  1. 3.Next to each image, the client writes what to do on a hard day: “call my sister after seven.”
  1. 4.In green, the client marks what they can do today.

Example (boy, 10 years old):

  • ▫“Grandma,” “the pool,” “building set,” “I don’t give up”
  • ▫next to Grandma - “call her after school,” next to the pool - “ask the coach what I’m already getting right.”

To close, the client takes a photo of the collage, returns the cards to the deck, and decides where to keep the sheet or the photo: on the fridge, on their phone.

For the facilitator: don’t interpret the cards for the client; you can help a child with the writing, but the words are the child’s own.
