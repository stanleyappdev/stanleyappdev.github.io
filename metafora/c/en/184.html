Art technique

My dream

  • Life path
  • Values
  • Your own wants
  • Circle of influence
  • Authoring your life

Goal: explore one specific wish, separate what depends on the client from what doesn’t, and decide on the first step.

Approximate time: 40-60 minutes.

Materials: 10-15 different magazines or printouts with pictures, an A3 sheet or cardboard, scissors, a glue stick, markers.

  1. 1.Choose the dream

Ask the client to name one specific wish rather than life in general: “Which wish matters most to you right now?” For example, moving, a new profession, a trip, a space of one’s own.

  1. 2.Make the collage

“Make a collage about this dream: what it will be like when it comes true.” Allow 20-25 minutes for choosing and gluing.

  1. 3.Look at the dream
  • ✓What is most important in the collage?
  • ✓What will you feel when it comes true?
  • ✓What in this dream is truly yours?
  1. 4.Separate what you can influence

Invite the client to circle in one color what depends on them and in another what doesn’t, or only partly does: money, other people, circumstances.

  • ✓What has already been done in this direction?
  • ✓What of the things that don’t depend on you can be prepared or found out?
  • ✓Who can help?
  1. 5.Decide on the first step

“What first step is possible this month?” Make it concrete: what, when, with whom.

With teenagers aged 15-18

Dreams may concern studies, hobbies, their own money, a trip. Discuss what the teenager can do independently and where adults’ consent or help is needed.

Note for the practitioner

Don’t judge how realistic the dream is. Even if it is out of reach now, you can find out what need it expresses and look for an available step in that direction.
