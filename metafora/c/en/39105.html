Animated film

My Neighbor Totoro

  • Children's fears
  • Anxiety
  • Support
  • Adapting to change

Sisters Satsuki and Mei move with their dad into an old house in the countryside to be closer to the hospital where their mother is recovering. The girls explore the house, get to know the neighbors and meet Totoro in the forest, a giant forest spirit whom only children can see. Satsuki tries to be grown-up: she cooks, looks after her younger sister and waits for their mom. The story is calm and has no villains.

The ending is revealed below. News comes from the hospital that Mom won’t be able to come home for the weekend as promised. Satsuki, who has been holding herself together all along, cries and admits to a neighbor that she is afraid for their mother. Little Mei sets off alone for the hospital with an ear of corn for Mom and gets lost. The whole village searches for her. Satsuki asks Totoro for help, and the Catbus takes her to her sister and then both of them to the hospital. The girls see their mother laughing with their dad and leave the corn on the windowsill.

Themes: a child’s anxiety about a parent’s illness; an older child who takes on adult duties; moving; support close at hand.

Who to offer it to: children from 5 when someone in the family is ill or in hospital, after a move, and older siblings who feel responsible for younger ones.

Questions

  • ✓Why did Satsuki hold on for so long and then burst into tears?
  • ✓What did Mei feel when she set off to Mom on her own?
  • ✓Who helped the sisters while Mom was in hospital?
  • ✓Do you sometimes worry about someone in your family? Who can you tell about it?
  • ✓If you had your own Totoro, how would it help you?
