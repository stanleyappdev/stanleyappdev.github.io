Exercise

Places where I might feel anxious

  • Children's fears
  • Anxiety
  • Adjustment
  • Uncertainty
  • Awareness of thoughts
  • Acting despite fear

KEY IDEA

thoughts are not facts

Helps the child identify the anxious moments of an upcoming event and work out what information they need.

How to run

  1. 1.Name the planned event and its stages.
  • ▫Example: the way to the club, arriving, meeting people, the session, going back home.
  1. 2.Fill in the form for the moment that worries the child.

A sample entry. Replace it with your own answers.

FieldSample answer
Place or momentWalking into the first club session.
What I expectI won’t know who to go up to.
What I already knowI know the time and the address.
What I want to find outWho will meet me at the entrance?
My first stepTogether with an adult, I’ll ask the organizer.
  1. 3.Separate known facts from assumptions. Agree on where to get the missing information.

Discussion questions

  • ✓What information does the child lack most?
  • ✓Which first step do they choose?
