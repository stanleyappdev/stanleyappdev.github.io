Exercise

Wants and needs: what’s behind my choice

  • Your own wants
  • Self-care
  • Decision-making
  • Right to choose
  • Values

Helps the child tell a need apart from a specific want and explain their choice.

How to run

  1. 1.Look through the examples. Next to each one, mark “need,” “want” or “depends on the situation,” and write down the child’s explanation.
