Game

Animal families

  • Team building
  • Nonverbal communication
  • Imagination
  • Active listening
  • Friendship

Goal: find your “own” people without words. Suitable for dividing into teams or as a warm-up.

Approximate time: 10-15 minutes.

Materials: cards with names or pictures of animals. The number of identical cards depends on the team size you need: two each for pairs, three or four each for small groups.

  1. 1.Hand out the cards

Everyone silently receives a card and shows it to nobody.

  1. 2.The search

On the signal, participants walk slowly around the room and act out their animal with movements or sounds, without naming it. Whoever recognizes their “own” stands beside them.

  1. 3.Checking

When everyone has found each other, the teams show their cards and check.

  1. 4.Making it harder

In the first round mime only; in the second round sounds only, from where you stand.

  1. 5.Version for small teams

Participants are given roles in an animal “family” (for example mother, father, baby), and once together the family lines up in an agreed order. The roles are invented and have nothing to do with real families.

  1. 6.Discuss
  • ✓Which signals helped you recognize your team?
  • ✓What was hard to show without words?
  1. 7.Close

Each team shows its animal together, and the others guess.

Safety

Move with eyes open, without pushing.

If someone mocks

If someone is laughed at for the way they act out their animal, stop: “Everyone shows it in their own way.” Support the participant and talk with the author separately.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widespread group game; the author is unknown. Instructions written by the Metafora editors.
