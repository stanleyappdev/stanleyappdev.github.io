Metaphor cards: a sketch of the week I want

  • Your own wants
  • Motivation
  • Authoring your life
  • Decision-making
  • Self-care

Obligations eat up the whole week, and personal wishes get postponed to “someday.” The exercise helps fit them into a real schedule. About 25 minutes.

What you need: an imagery deck, 30 cards face up; a sheet divided by hand into seven columns for the days of the week.

How to run

  1. 1.The client chooses three cards face up: “What do I want more of in my life?” “What do I want less of?” “What already supports me?” For each one, ask: “What is happening here? What does this image mean to you?”
  1. 2.Turn each image into something concrete. A card with a bicycle - “more movement: two 30-minute walks.” A card with a crowded street - “fewer evenings in work chats.”
  1. 3.On the sheet, first mark what cannot be moved: work, commuting, time with the children. Now the free slots are visible.
  1. 4.Fit in one or two actions from “more,” remove one task from “less,” and keep what already supports you. Leave one slot empty for rest or the unexpected.

To close, the client takes a photo of the “more” card as a reminder. At the next session, look at what worked and simplify what didn’t. In a group, each person plans their own week and then shares one idea in a pair.

For the facilitator: don’t interpret the cards for the client.
