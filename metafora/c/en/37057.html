Worksheet

My emotional needs

  • Your own wants
  • Self-care
  • Closeness

A review of how well your core emotional needs are being met now, not in the past. It is done at the start of therapy and repeated every few months to see the changes.

How to fill it in

Rate each need from 0 to 10 and write down two or three examples from the past month.

  1. 1.Secure attachment and acceptance: are there people with whom you feel at ease, don’t have to pretend and can rely on?
  1. 2.Autonomy and competence: do you make decisions yourself, and do you manage your own affairs?
  1. 3.Freedom to express needs and emotions: can you say “I’m sad,” “I want,” “this doesn’t work for me,” and what happens then?
  1. 4.Spontaneity and play: when did you last do something simply because it was interesting, not because it was useful?
  1. 5.Realistic limits: can you stop, keep agreements with yourself, tolerate someone else’s “no”?

After rating

- Which need do you sacrifice first when you are short of time or energy?

- What small step can you take this week for your most neglected need? For example: “Call my sister on Sunday.”

For the facilitator: in a group, participants fill in the worksheet on their own and discuss only what they are ready to share, without comparing ratings.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

Core emotional needs as described by Jeffrey Young; J. Young, J. Klosko, M. Weishaar, Schema Therapy: A Practitioner’s Guide (2003).
