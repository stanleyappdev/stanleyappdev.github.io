Exercise

Shared tale candle

  • Friendship
  • Imagination
  • Active listening
  • Team building
  • Communication

Sit in a circle. The first participant switches on an LED candle or holds a paper “light” and starts a made-up tale with one or two sentences. Then they pass the light to the next person, who continues it. The story travels around the whole circle this way.

Anyone can say “pass” and hand on their turn. The last participant or the whole group comes up with the ending. Use only a flameless light or a paper symbol.

Discuss how the story changed thanks to everyone’s contribution and what “sharing warmth” meant in this game.

“Tale with a Ball” variation

The person holding a soft ball starts a tale with a few phrases, then names the next storyteller and passes them the ball. The next person continues the story and passes the turn on. If the group agrees, the turn can be passed mid-sentence, leaving the next person an unexpected continuation.

Choose how it will end in advance: after an agreed time, after a certain number of passes or when the story reaches a logical end. The ball can be rolled or replaced with a gesture; skipping a turn is allowed.
