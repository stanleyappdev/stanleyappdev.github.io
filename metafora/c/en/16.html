Metaphor

The lighthouse keeper

  • Meaning in life
  • Vocation
  • Self-worth
  • Self-care

Every night the lighthouse keeper cleans the lamp and makes sure the light doesn’t go out. The keeper almost never sees the ships this helps: they pass far off in the dark and never say thank you. But without his work, they would have a much harder time finding their way.

When to use it: the client doubts the value of their work because they don’t see its results, for example,

  • ▫parents
  • ▫teachers
  • ▫helping professionals.

Questions

  • ✓Who might be your “ships” that you don’t see?
  • ✓How do you know your light is needed if no one says thank you?
  • ✓What helps you tend your own “lamp” so you don’t burn out?
