Worksheet

My calendar of holidays and little joys

  • Joy
  • Your own wants
  • Values
  • Closeness
  • Emotional connection
  • Self-care

Goal: notice the events that make the year warmer and gather them in one place.

Approximate time: 20-30 minutes.

Materials: a sheet divided into 12 parts for the months, or an ordinary wall calendar, pencils, markers, stickers.

  1. 1.Recall events

The child recalls what is special for them at different times of the year: birthdays, family customs, trips, get-togethers, the first snow, their own small victories.

  1. 2.Fill in the calendar

The child writes or draws each event next to the right month. Empty months stay empty; they can be filled in later.

  1. 3.Decorate the sheet

The sheet is decorated with colors and symbols.

Discussion questions

  • ✓Which event are you looking forward to most?
  • ✓What makes it special?
  • ✓What new tradition could be added to an empty month?
  • ✓Which of these can you arrange yourself or with friends?

Closing

Choose one small joy that can be arranged in the coming month and agree who will help. The calendar can be hung up at home and added to.

In a group

Everyone makes their own calendar; those who want show one month. Remind the group that celebrations differ from family to family and there’s no need to compare them.

With teens aged 11-14

Suggest adding events the teen can organize themselves: meeting friends, a personal project, a hike.

Note for the practitioner

If a loss, a move or the war has changed the family’s usual celebrations, the sheet may touch on sadness. Leave room for what used to be and don’t insist on happy examples.
