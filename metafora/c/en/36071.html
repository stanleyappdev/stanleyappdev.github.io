Metaphor

The elephant on a thin rope

  • Helplessness
  • Authoring your life
  • Inner support

A baby elephant is tied to a stake with a rope. It tugs for a day, a second day, a week, but the stake won’t budge. Eventually the baby elephant stops trying. Years go by, the elephant grows up and could rip the stake right out of the ground, yet it stays tied by the same rope. What holds it now is not the rope but the memory that pulling is useless.

Questions

  • ✓What “rope” has been holding you since the time when you really were smaller and weaker?
  • ✓What has changed since then: your age, strength, knowledge, the people around you?
  • ✓How could you test the stake safely - not by yanking with all your might, but by giving it a gentle pull?

Note: this metaphor is not about the person “holding themselves back.” Once, the rope was real, and caution made sense at the time. The point is that conditions may have changed.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A popular teaching story; author unknown. It is offered as an image, not as a description of how elephants are actually trained. Free retelling by Metafora.
