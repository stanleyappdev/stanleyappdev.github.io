Exercise

10 minutes without stimulation

  • Boredom
  • Presence
  • Awareness of thoughts

“Without stimulation” here means without an extra feed, music, or task - not complete sensory isolation. Choose a safe place where you don’t have to keep an eye on the road, children, or work.

Set a timer for up to 10 minutes. You can start with one or two minutes; increasing the time is optional. Sit comfortably, keep your eyes open, and put your phone aside.

Notice thoughts, sounds, sensations, and the urge to switch something on. You can name them silently: “planning,” “remembering,” “want to check messages.” There’s no need to clear your head or hold on to calm. If tension rises noticeably, end the trial and go back to your usual activity.

When the timer goes off, write down: what was hardest, what surprised you, and whether it was really stimulation you needed or something else.
