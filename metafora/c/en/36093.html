Metaphor

Two coaches

  • Self-compassion
  • Self-criticism
  • Motivation

Two young runners fall during practice. The first coach shouts, “Again! You’re hopeless, you’ll never amount to anything.” The second comes over, helps the runner up, and says, “Does it hurt? Let’s take a look. You caught your foot on the edge of the track; next time watch where you’re stepping. Rest for a minute, and then we’ll run again.”

Both coaches want their runners to get faster. But one runner will soon become afraid to run, while the other will keep training.

The metaphor helps those who believe that without harsh self-criticism they’ll become lazy. Self-compassion is not giving up on standards but a different way of setting them.

Questions

  • ✓Which coach do you hear in your head after a mistake? Whose voice does it speak in?
  • ✓What would the second coach say about your most recent setback?
  • ✓Which of the two would you want to train with for the next year?
