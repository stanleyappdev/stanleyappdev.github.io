Exercise

The story in reverse

  • Active listening
  • Attention and memory
  • Team building

The first volunteer tells a short made-up story of several events. The second retells it starting from the end: first the last event, then the one before it, and so on back to the beginning. The point is the order of events, not saying the words backward.

Here is an example:

  • ▫“The girl took an umbrella, went outside, and met a friend.”

The reverse retelling: “She met a friend; before that she went outside; and before that she took an umbrella.”

Start with three events. The reteller may ask to hear the original story again, or you can help with the question “What happened before that?” Then swap roles.

Discuss what helped to remember the sequence: a mental picture, separate words, or how the events were linked. Don’t compare participants’ abilities.
