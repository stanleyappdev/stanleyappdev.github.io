Art technique

Mine - not mine

  • Personal boundaries
  • Your own wants
  • Right to choose
  • Self-worth
  • Safety

Goal: tell one’s own thoughts and wishes apart from what comes from outside, and practice protecting one’s boundary.

Approximate time: 25-35 minutes.

Materials: a large sheet, pencils; magazine cut-outs and glue if wished.

  1. 1.Outline your space

In the center of the sheet the child draws themselves or their symbol and draws a circle around it: the boundary of personal space.

  1. 2.Fill the circle

Inside the circle the child adds what they consider their own: favorite activities, dreams, feelings, decisions.

  1. 3.Mark what comes from outside

Outside the boundary they place what they hear from others: advice, demands, judgments, nicknames.

  1. 4.Look at the outer part
  • ✓What of this is useful and can be let in?
  • ✓What is not about you at all?
  • ✓What of this do you hear most often? How do you feel then?
  1. 5.Mark what protects the boundary

Next to the boundary the child draws what helps protect it: the word “no,” a person to turn to, a phrase of their own.

  1. 6.Close

Choose one phrase the child can say when they hear something that is “not about me,” and rehearse it.

With teenagers

Outside there may be parents’ expectations, peers’ opinions, social media. Question: “What of this did you choose yourself, and what did you accept to avoid arguing?”

Note for the practitioner

If signs of abuse appear outside the boundary, give this separate attention and take care of the child’s safety.
