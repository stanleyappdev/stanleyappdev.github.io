Metaphor

Boundaries as a fence with a gate

  • Personal boundaries
  • Closeness
  • Right to choose

A fence shows where your garden begins. A gate lets you invite guests in, agree on a time, and close it when you need rest. A boundary does not have to make the garden inaccessible; it helps organize contact.

In relationships, the gate can be an agreement - “I’m glad to help if we settle on a time” - or a refusal: “I’m not having guests today.” Other people have their own gardens too, and the right to decide whom to let in.

Questions

  • ✓Where is my fence unclear?
  • ✓Do I end up building a high wall straight away because I don’t know how to use the gate?
  • ✓What terms of contact work for me?

Phrase one specific agreement. The metaphor does not require letting everyone in: access can be restricted for someone who repeatedly violates boundaries.
