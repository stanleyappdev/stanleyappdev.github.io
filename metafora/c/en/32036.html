Exercise

Guess and check

  • Imagination
  • Communication
  • Inner support
  • Decision-making

An exercise on how a guess differs from a checked fact.

First, play with simple questions about the room: how many windows, how many people in light-colored clothes, how many chairs. Everyone makes a guess, then you check together.

Next, each child picks someone in the group (with their consent) and writes one friendly question for them, for example about their favorite season. Next to it, the child writes how they think the person will answer and marks it as a guess. Then they ask the question and compare.

Discussion questions

  • ✓why our guesses about others are sometimes wrong;
  • ✓how you can check what another person thinks;
  • ✓why it matters to ask rather than decide for someone.
