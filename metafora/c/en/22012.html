Exercise

Places where I feel anxious

  • Children's fears
  • Anxiety
  • Body awareness
  • Awareness of thoughts
  • Overwhelm
  • Presence

Helps the child identify which places and circumstances are linked to their anxiety.

How to run

  1. 1.Invite the child to recall one or two recent situations and fill in the form for each.
FieldAnswer
Where and when was it?
What was happening?
What exactly worried me?
Have I felt calmer in this place? What was different then?

Discussion questions

  • ✓What do these situations have in common?
  • ✓Is the anxiety linked to the place itself or to what happens there?

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

Theoretical basis: NHS, “Anxiety in children.”
