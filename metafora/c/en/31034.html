Game

Thumb wrestling

  • Coordination
  • Personal boundaries
  • Self-regulation
  • Attention and memory

Goal: practice combining excitement with care, following rules, and respectful physical contact in a pair.

Approximate time: 10 minutes.

Materials: none needed.

  1. 1.Form pairs

Only volunteers form pairs. A participant who doesn’t want contact plays the no-grip version or acts as referee.

  1. 2.Show the grip

The leader shows a light grip linking the four fingers of the right hand; the thumbs stay free and point upward.

  1. 3.Rules
  • ✓The task: briefly touch your partner’s thumb with your own while avoiding theirs.
  • ✓Don’t press the thumb down hard, jerk the hand or twist the joints.
  • ✓On the word “stop”, let go at once.
  1. 4.Rounds

Play a short round of 30-60 seconds, thank your partner and change partners if you like.

  1. 5.Version without a grip

Follow your partner’s movements in the air with your finger.

  1. 6.Discuss
  • ✓How did you manage to combine excitement and care?
  • ✓How did you know your partner was comfortable?
  1. 7.Close

Pairs thank each other. No totals of “who won how many” are announced.

Note for the practitioner

If the game gets rough, stop the pair and remind them of the rules; a participant who is hurt leaves without having to explain.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widespread group game; the author is unknown. Instructions written by the Metafora editors.
