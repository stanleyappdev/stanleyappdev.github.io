Art technique

My strength

  • Fear
  • Safety
  • Children's self-esteem
  • Self-confidence
  • Inner support
  • Self-presentation

Goal: create an image of one’s own strength and link it to real situations where this strength has already shown itself.

Approximate time: 25-40 minutes.

Materials: plasticine or clay, a board; natural materials if wished: pebbles, cones, twigs.

  1. 1.Find the image

“Think about your strength: what helps you endure, act, not give up. What could it be like if you could hold it in your hands?” It can be an animal, an object, an abstract shape.

  1. 2.Model it

Allow 10-15 minutes. Details from natural materials can be added.

  1. 3.Look at it
  • ✓Tell me about your strength. What is it like?
  • ✓Which part of the figure is the most important?
  • ✓When do you feel this strength?
  • ✓Where does it come from?
  1. 4.Find the strength in life

“Recall a specific situation when this strength showed itself.” Then: “Where will it be needed soon?”

  1. 5.Close

The client gives the figure a name and decides where to keep it. The figure can be photographed as a reminder.

In a group

Everyone models their own figure. Then those who wish take turns briefly presenting it (1-2 minutes), and the others say what they noticed, without judging whether it is “nice.” Anyone who doesn’t want to show it can say just one word about their strength. The leader stops a hurtful comment at once and restores the rule of respectful feedback; the circumstances are clarified separately.

With a child aged 3-6

“Make something strong that is like you.” Questions: “What can your animal do? When are you this strong?”
