Metaphor

The unpaid bill

  • Resentment

An old grievance hangs on like a bill issued to another person: “You owe me.” We keep it in plain sight, check every day whether payment has arrived, and meanwhile the debt keeps accruing interest. The debtor may not even know about the bill, or may have nothing to pay with. The one who wears out is the one who waits.

Closing the bill doesn’t mean admitting there was no debt. It means no longer paying it with your own peace of mind.

Questions

  • ✓What exactly are you “owed” in this story: an apology, acknowledgment, lost time, justice?
  • ✓Could this person ever settle the account?
  • ✓What does it cost you to keep the bill open?
