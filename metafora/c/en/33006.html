Game

A conversation without “yes” and “no”

  • Self-regulation
  • Communication
  • Attention and memory

Goal: practice flexible speech: looking for more precise words instead of the usual “yes” and “no”.

Approximate time: 15-20 minutes.

Participants: 6 or more, in pairs or mingling freely.

Materials: 5 large paper tokens per person (you can cut 5×5 cm squares out of colored card).

  1. 1.Explain the rules

During the round you do not use the words “yes” and “no”. You can answer questions in other ways: “I agree”, “Not today”, “I choose something else”. Ask neutral questions: about the weather, food, hobbies. “I’ll skip that” is always allowed.

  1. 2.The conversation

Participants talk in pairs and change partners at the leader’s signal (every 2 minutes). If a forbidden word slips out, the participant gives one token to their partner. Someone who has run out of tokens keeps playing and can win them back under the same rule.

  1. 3.Pace

If you like, agree on a pause of about five seconds before answering. Skip this rule for a group that needs more time.

Discussion

  • ✓Which substitutes were easy to find?
  • ✓How did you react to a mistake, your own and others’?
  • ✓What helped you stay friendly?

Closing

Count the tokens without announcing winners; everyone names one substitute they liked.

If someone mocks

You may not deliberately “trap” someone with questions that put them on the spot, or ask personal questions. If this happens, stop the pair, return the tokens and explain the rule.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widely played group game; no author identified. The description of the rules, the discussion questions and the closing are editorial.
