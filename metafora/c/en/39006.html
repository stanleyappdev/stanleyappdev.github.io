Lady Bird

  • Who am I?
  • Conflict
  • Upbringing
  • Friendship
  • Shame and guilt

Christine McPherson (Saoirse Ronan) insists that everyone call her Lady Bird: she gave herself the name. It is 2002, she is in her final year at a Catholic school in Sacramento, a city she finds boring, and she dreams of getting into a college on the East Coast. Her mother Marion (Laurie Metcalf), a nurse, works double shifts because her father Larry has lost his job and is struggling with depression. Marion believes the family can’t afford such a college and constantly criticises her daughter. They can cry peacefully together over an audiobook in the car and a moment later quarrel so badly that Lady Bird jumps out of the moving car and breaks her arm. Over the year she falls for Danny, who turns out to be gay, then for the conceited Kyle, drops her best friend Julie for the popular Jenna, is ashamed of her neighbourhood and lies about it. Her father secretly helps her apply for financial aid.

Spoiler: Lady Bird gets into a college in New York. Her mother is hurt that she was the last to find out, stops speaking to her and doesn’t even say goodbye at the airport, then turns back but is too late. Her father puts letters into his daughter’s suitcase that Marion wrote but didn’t dare to give. In New York the girl introduces herself as Christine and leaves her mother a voicemail thanking her.

Psychological thread: separation from the family, in which love and conflict are closely intertwined; the search for one’s own self through a name, friends, a city. Themes: mother-daughter relationships, shame about poverty, friendship, first sexual experience, growing up.

Who to suggest it to: teens from 16 and parents, especially mothers and daughters before college, a move or separation; adults rethinking their relationship with their mother.

Caution: teenage sex scenes, swearing, parties with alcohol and drugs. Agree on viewing with a teen with their parents.

Questions

  • ✓Why did Christine name herself Lady Bird? What does it mean to choose for yourself what you will be called?
  • ✓Remember the scene in the car. How does a calm conversation turn into a quarrel? What is each of them trying to say?
  • ✓What is Marion afraid of when she criticises her daughter? What does Lady Bird long for?
  • ✓Why does Lady Bird drop Julie, and what brings her back to her friend?
  • ✓What changes in Lady Bird when she is far from home? What from home does every person carry with them?
