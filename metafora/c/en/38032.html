Symboldrama: Tree

  • Inner support
  • Who am I?
  • Resources

The person imagines a tree that appears by itself, looks at it and describes how it stands and what it rests on.

Approximate time: 30-40 minutes, of which 15-20 minutes in the imagery. Running it requires training in symboldrama (katathym imaginative psychotherapy).

How to run

  1. 1.A comfortable position, eyes closed or gaze lowered, a minute or two of calm breathing.
  1. 2.“Imagine a tree - whichever one appears first. Don’t choose it.”
  1. 3.What is it like, and where does it stand: alone in a field, among other trees, next to a house? The crown, the bark, the season.
  1. 4.Walk up to it, touch the bark, lean your back against the trunk. Feel how deep the roots go.

Questions

  • ✓How does the tree feel standing in its place?
  • ✓What do you feel as you lean against the trunk?

What to watch for

- Is the tree straight or leaning, dry, solitary? Does it offer support?

- Don’t “heal” the tree or decode its parts.

How to close

Lean against the trunk once more, remember the feeling of support and say goodbye. Feel your feet, take a deeper breath, stretch and open your eyes. What was most vivid? Suggest drawing the tree at home.

In a group, everyone imagines in silence following the facilitator’s instructions and then draws; sharing in pairs is optional, and no one interprets other people’s trees.

Caution: a dry or broken tree can be upsetting, but it is not a “bad sign.” The client can stop at any moment. If anxiety is strong, help them return to a safe place within the image, or ask them to open their eyes and feel their feet. Do not use in an acute crisis or with people who have difficulty telling the imagined from the real.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

An additional motif in symboldrama (Hanscarl Leuner’s katathym imaginative psychotherapy).
