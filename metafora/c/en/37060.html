Exercise

What would the Healthy Adult say?

  • Decision-making
  • Supportive self-talk
  • Responsibility

An exercise for a specific difficult decision: refusing to lend your brother money, responding to an offer to relocate. First the inner voices are heard out, then the Healthy Adult’s answer is formulated and tested with three questions.

How to run

  1. 1.Describe the situation in two or three sentences.
  1. 2.Write one line from each part. The critic: “If you say no, you’re selfish.” The Vulnerable Child: “I’m scared my brother will take offense.” The Detached Protector: “Don’t think about it, put it off.”
  1. 3.Formulate the Healthy Adult’s answer and test it:

- Is it realistic? Does it take the facts into account rather than the worst- or best-case scenario?

- Is it caring? Does it consider your feelings and needs as well as other people’s feelings?

- Is it responsible? Does it lead to an action whose consequences you are prepared to own?

If the answer fails even one of these tests, rewrite it.

Example

  • ▫“I’m scared to say no, and that’s understandable. I can’t lend the money right now: I wouldn’t have enough for rent. I’ll tell my brother honestly and offer to help in another way.”
  1. 4.Decide on the first step and when you will take it.

If an answer won’t come together, ask yourself: “What would someone who knows me well, respects me and isn’t afraid of the truth say?”
