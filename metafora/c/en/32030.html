Game

Far, far away, in a deep forest…

  • Imagination
  • Active listening
  • Self-regulation
  • Team building
  • Negotiation

Goal: practice listening, agreeing on which idea to choose and building a story together.

Approximate time: 15-20 minutes.

Participants: 5-15 in a circle.

Materials: if you like, a soft toy passed to whoever is speaking.

  1. 1.Explain the rules

“We are going to make up a story together. I start a sentence and you suggest how it continues. Whoever holds the toy speaks.” If several ideas come up, the group chooses one, and the others may come in useful later.

  1. 2.Build the story

The leader begins: “Far, far away, in a thick forest…” Someone suggests a hero. The leader repeats what has been made up so far and adds a new opening: “…and one morning he decided…”, “…when suddenly…”. Make sure those who speak more quietly get a turn.

  1. 3.The ending

When the story gets too long, make up an ending together. The leader retells the whole story.

Discussion

  • ✓How did you agree which idea to take?
  • ✓What was it like when your idea was not chosen?
  • ✓What helped give everyone a turn?

Closing

Everyone names their favorite moment of the story. If you like, it can be written down and drawn in the next session.

If someone mocks

If the story is turned into a joke about someone present, stop it: “The characters are made up.” We don’t laugh at ideas: an idea that was not chosen can be used later.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widely played group game; no author identified. The description of the rules, the discussion questions and the closing are editorial.
