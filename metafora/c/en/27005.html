What we have in common: a group icebreaker

  • Friendship
  • Communication
  • Adjustment
  • Self-presentation
  • Right to choose
  • Team building

Goal: help group members get to know each other through shared likes and experiences.

Approximate time: 20-30 minutes.

Materials: a printed sheet for each participant, pens; a timer.

How to run

  1. 1.Prepare the sheet

Look through the questions with the group or in advance and replace any that don’t fit. Items about travel can hurt children who have not had that opportunity; replacements are given under the table.

  1. 2.Explain the rules

“In 10 minutes, find people who answer ‘yes’ to the questions and write down their names. Ask one participant one question at a time, then move on to someone else. Answering is optional; you can say ‘pass’. We are not competing to see who fills the sheet first.”

  1. 3.The game

Participants walk around the room, ask each other and, with consent, write down names. The leader makes sure nobody is left out and, if needed, goes over to participants nobody has talked to yet.

Find someone who…Names
Loves chocolate
Loves cakes
Has traveled by plane
Loves drawing
Has traveled abroad
Loves dogs
Loves kittens
Loves apples
Loves singing
Loves reading
  • ▫Replacements for the travel items: loves going for walks, is curious about other cities, loves board games.
  1. 4.Wrap-up in the circle

The leader reads out 3-4 items and asks those they fit to raise a hand, so everyone sees what they share.

Discussion questions

  • ✓Which shared trait surprised you?
  • ✓Who did you learn something new about?
  • ✓Who would you like to talk with longer?
  1. 5.Close

Everyone names one person they found something in common with, and what it is.

If someone mocks

If someone’s answer is laughed at (“You love kittens?”), stop: “Stop. All likes are fine here.” Support the participant and talk with the author separately. Don’t compare how many names people collected.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

“What We Have in Common” worksheet, Rozvytok Dytyny (Child Development) project, 2019 (in Ukrainian).
