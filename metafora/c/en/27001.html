How anger feels in the body

  • Children's emotions
  • Anger
  • Body awareness
  • Self-regulation
  • Self-soothing
  • Recognizing and naming emotions
  • Aggression in children

Helps the child notice their own bodily signs of anger.

How to run

  1. 1.Ask the child to recall a situation they can already talk about calmly.
  1. 2.Fill in the worksheet or mark the sensations on a body outline.

  1. 3.Ask the child to mark one or two signs that appear earliest.

Discussion questions

  • ✓Which signal does the child notice first?
  • ✓Which sensations come back in other situations when the child is angry?
