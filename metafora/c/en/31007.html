Game

Copy the funny move

  • Imagination
  • Coordination
  • Self-regulation
  • Presence

Goal: practice imitation, body awareness and switching roles between “I show” and “I repeat”.

Approximate time: 10 minutes.

Materials: free space so that everyone can spread their arms.

  1. 1.Prepare the space

Before the game make sure everyone has enough room and there are no sharp corners nearby.

  1. 2.Explain the rules

The leader shows a simple movement and explains whom it represents: a bird flapping its wings, a driver turning the wheel, a painter painting a fence, a bear waddling. The group repeats it.

  1. 3.Change of leader

Then one of the children becomes the leader. The leader can show a movement without explaining, and the group guesses whom it represents. Movements can be done sitting down or with the hands only.

  1. 4.Warm-up variation

The leader puts several images together into a short funny sequence: a tree swaying in the wind, a plane humming, a windmill turning its sails. The group repeats it together.

  1. 5.Close

Finally, everyone shows the movement they liked most, and everyone repeats it.

With children aged 3-6

One movement at a time; the leader does it together with the children.

With children aged 7-10

A sequence of 3-4 movements that must be remembered and repeated in the same order.

If someone mocks

We copy a movement, not a person: mimicking someone’s walk, voice or particular features is not allowed. If it happens, stop the game, support the child and talk to the author separately.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widespread children’s game; the author is unknown. Instructions written by the Metafora editors.
