Exercise

The secret letter

  • Expressing emotions
  • Your own wants
  • Self-care
  • Supportive self-talk
  • Personal boundaries

Goal: give space to words that are hard to say aloud.

Approximate time: 20-30 minutes.

Materials: paper, a pen or pencils, an envelope, stickers for decorating.

  1. 1.Choose the addressee

The participant decides whom the letter is for: themselves in a year, someone they miss, an imaginary friend or a favorite animal.

  1. 2.Write or draw

The participant writes or draws what they want to convey. A few lines or symbols are enough.

  1. 3.Seal it

The sheet is decorated and folded into an envelope.

  1. 4.Discuss without reading the letter
  • ✓What was it like to write?
  • ✓What was hardest to say?
  • ✓What do you feel now?
  1. 5.Decide the letter’s fate

The participant decides what happens to the letter next: keep it, hide it, give it, or come back to it later.

Note for the practitioner

The practitioner doesn’t ask to see or read the letter. If the letter is addressed to someone who has been lost, leave time after the exercise for conversation and check how the child is feeling.
