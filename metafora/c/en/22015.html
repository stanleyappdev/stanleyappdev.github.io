Exercise

Action plan for when I feel anxious

  • Anxiety
  • Self-regulation
  • Awareness of thoughts
  • Decision-making
  • Making requests
  • Recognizing and naming emotions

Helps make a concrete plan in advance for a recurring worrying situation.

How to run

  1. 1.Choose a situation together with the child and fill in the form.

A sample entry. Replace it with your own answers.

FieldSample answer
When do I feel anxious?When I don’t understand a task at my club.
What do I tell myself?Everyone else already gets it, and I don’t.
What do I feel?I feel nervous; I clench my hands.
What do I usually do?I stay quiet and put the work aside.
What will I try this time?I’ll say: “Could you please explain the first step?”
Who will help?The club leader.
If the step is too big, I……will first point to the part I don’t understand.
  1. 2.Agree on where the child will keep the plan. After they try it, discuss what was helpful and what needs to be made simpler.

Discussion questions

  • ✓Is it clear what to do first?
  • ✓What support is needed from an adult?
