Metaphor

The jigsaw puzzle

  • Who am I?
  • Meaning in life
  • Uncertainty
  • Inner support

KEY IDEA

thoughts are not facts

Imagine putting together a jigsaw puzzle without the box lid: there is no finished picture to check against. At first little is clear. Some people start with the straight edges because they are easy to recognise; others gather pieces of the same colour. Each piece you place shows a little more of what the picture is. Some pieces find no place for a long time and then suddenly fit.

The missing lid is like a situation in which a person does not know how changes, treatment or a new stage of life will turn out. The edges are what they are already sure of. Single pieces are events and observations whose meaning is not yet clear.

When to use it: at the start of therapy, in periods of uncertainty, when a client demands of themselves to know the whole plan at once or dismisses small steps.

Questions

  • ✓What in your situation is like the straight edges, something you are already sure of?
  • ✓Which piece have you recently put in place?
  • ✓Which piece doesn’t fit anywhere yet? What if you set it aside for now?
  • ✓What is already showing in the picture, even though it isn’t finished?

Note for the practitioner

Do not promise that the picture will necessarily come together into a harmonious whole. The metaphor is about understanding that can come gradually.
