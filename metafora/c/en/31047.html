Game

Keep the rhythm and pass the name

  • Team building
  • Coordination
  • Friendship
  • Self-regulation
  • Attention and memory

Goal: remember participants’ names and practice attention and a shared rhythm.

Approximate time: 10-15 minutes.

Materials: none needed.

  1. 1.Set the rhythm

Everyone sits in a circle and together keeps a four-count rhythm: two pats on the knees, two claps. First just keep the rhythm until it is steady.

  1. 2.Passing a name

On the knee pats the participant says their own name, on the claps the name of the person they pass the turn to. The person named picks it up on the next cycle of the rhythm. Long names can be shortened however the person prefers.

  1. 3.Version with numbers

Instead of names, everyone gets a number according to their place in the circle. Pass the turn by numbers, first in order, then in any order. When the group is confident, speed up the tempo slightly.

  1. 4.Mistakes

If the rhythm breaks, nobody is out: slow down and start again. Make sure the turn reaches everyone and doesn’t circle among a few participants.

  1. 5.Close

Check whose names people remember: volunteers name everyone in the circle. Ask what helped them keep the rhythm.

If someone mocks

Names are not mimicked. If someone does it, stop the rhythm: “Stop. We don’t make fun of names here.” Check how the participant is and talk with the author separately.
