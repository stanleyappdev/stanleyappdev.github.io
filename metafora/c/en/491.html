Children’s online safety

  • Safety
  • Upbringing
  • Trust
  • Personal boundaries

Three pillars: agreements rather than total control; openness, so the child will come to you after something unpleasant; age restrictions and privacy settings.

The main rule worth stating directly: if someone online asks to keep the contact secret from parents, to send photos, or to meet alone, that is a reason to tell an adult-and the child will never be punished for it.

With screen time, what matters is not the amount but what exactly the child is doing and whether it crowds out sleep, movement, and face-to-face contact.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

Editorial text by Metafora summarising common guidance on children’s online safety (including UNICEF and NSPCC materials); there is no single author.
