Metaphor

Envy as a flashlight

  • Your own wants
  • Jealousy and envy
  • Presence
  • Vocation

In a dark storeroom, a flashlight suddenly lit up a box of paints. The light didn’t create the paints, and it didn’t order anyone to become an artist right away. It only helped the owner see something they had been walking past for a long time.

Envy of someone else’s creativity can be used in a similar way: to take a closer look at a forgotten interest. But the beam is narrow. It doesn’t show the other person’s whole life, their difficulties, or your own other options.

Imagine what your flashlight is pointing at. Name the wish in one sentence and widen the light: what other ways are there to meet it? Choose a small trial - ten minutes of drawing or a conversation with someone from the field that interests you. After the trial you can change your mind; envy alone doesn’t determine your calling.
