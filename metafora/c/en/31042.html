Exercise

Swaying in place: follow the leader

  • Trust
  • Body awareness
  • Personal boundaries
  • Self-regulation

A calm exercise for balance and imitating movement.

The group stands in a circle. A volunteer steps into the middle, places their feet shoulder-width apart and keeps their eyes open. They begin to sway slowly and barely noticeably - forward, to the side, in a circle - like a tree in a light breeze. Everyone else, staying in place, copies these movements as precisely as possible.

No one touches the person in the middle. They decide for themselves how widely to sway and when to stop. The exercise can be done sitting on chairs.

After one or two leaders, ask: What helped you stay steady? How can you copy someone else’s movement without rushing?

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A version of a widely used group exercise; no specific author has been identified. The instructions are editorial.
