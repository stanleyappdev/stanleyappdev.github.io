Art technique

My source of light

  • Inner support
  • Resources
  • Self-soothing
  • Body awareness

Goal: notice an inner resource and what supports or weakens it.

Approximate time: 15-25 minutes.

Materials: a sheet of paper, paints or colored pencils; a sheet with a simple body outline if wished.

  1. 1.Invite the image

“Imagine a little flame burning inside you. What is it like: big or small, warm or cool, calm or flickering?”

  1. 2.Draw the flame

The child draws it in any shape and color. A simple body outline can be drawn next to it to mark where this light is felt.

  1. 3.Ask about it
  • ✓Tell me about your flame.
  • ✓What makes it brighter? Who or what supports it?
  • ✓What dims it?
  • ✓Where in your body do you feel it?

The answers can be written or marked with small signs next to the drawing.

  1. 4.Close

Choose one thing that supports the flame and agree when the child will do it soon.

With a child aged 3-6

It is enough to draw the flame and answer: “What makes your flame warmer?” The adult writes it down.

With teenagers

You can talk not about a flame but about a “charge” or a “battery”: what charges you, what drains you.

Note for the practitioner

If the image of light doesn’t come, any other symbol of strength will do: a tree, a stone, an animal. A small or dim flame is not a conclusion about the child’s state but a reason to ask what is happening with it.
