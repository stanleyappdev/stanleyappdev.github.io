Metaphor

Calling as a river

  • Vocation
  • Life path
  • Adapting to change
  • Values

A river is not the same width and speed everywhere. It meets stones, branches out, takes in tributaries. If you look for it only where it was ten years ago, you may miss its present course.

This is one way to think about an important direction in life: it can change shape while keeping certain values, or open up new ones. At the same time, the riverbed is limited by real-life conditions; not every obstacle can be overcome by wanting alone.

Draw the river of your interests. Mark the tributaries - experiences and people; the backwaters - pauses; the banks - limitations and support.

Questions

  • ✓Where do I want to direct my energy now?
  • ✓What can’t I change on my own?
  • ✓Where do I need help?

Choose one small move without demanding that you see the whole way to the sea.
