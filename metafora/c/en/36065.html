Metaphor

The train is late

  • Circle of influence
  • Control
  • Uncertainty

The train has been standing in the middle of a field for over an hour. One passenger refreshes the app every five minutes, writes angry reviews and argues with the driver in his head. The passenger across from him is upset too: she is about to miss an important meeting. She calls and reschedules it, asks the attendant for some tea, takes out a book, dozes a little.

The train arrives for both of them at the same time. But they step off the car in completely different states.

The metaphor is not about not getting upset: both of them have every reason to be frustrated. It is about where your energy goes while you wait.

Questions

  • ✓What in your life right now is like a train that can’t be made to go faster?
  • ✓What are you doing “in the car”? What of this gives you energy, and what drains it?
  • ✓What call, request or act of self-care is possible for you today?
