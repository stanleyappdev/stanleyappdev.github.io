Art technique

Group drawing

  • Communication
  • Roles
  • Negotiation
  • Active listening
  • Friendship
  • Team building
  • Imagination

The group draws on a single sheet with no prior agreement. Then they discuss who took the center, who waited, who added to others’ drawings.

Children’s variants: “Our group” and “A picture to remember”

On a shared large sheet, each person adds something to the image of the group with their own marker. Agree on a space for each person and on a rule: someone else’s contribution can be changed only with its author’s consent. If they wish, participants explain what they drew.

Discuss: “What was it like drawing side by side? What did you feel while listening to others? What new things did you learn about the group? What would you like to thank someone for?”

To close the session, suggest “A picture to remember”: each person adds an image of an important moment from today’s work. Together, come up with a title and agree where the drawing will be kept.

Variant with passing sheets

On their own sheet, each person starts drawing something important to them that they are willing to share. At a signal, they pass the sheet to the right and receive the drawing of the neighbor on their left. Add details without crossing out anyone else’s contribution; agree in advance that such additions are allowed.

Continue until each sheet returns to the person who started it. If the group wishes, hold an exhibition and talk about how the original ideas changed. Works are displayed only with the consent of those who made them.
