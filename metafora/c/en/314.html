Mood barometer

  • Feedback
  • Active listening
  • Awareness of emotions
  • Team building

Goal: at the start of a meeting, briefly mark your mood and see what the group has come with today.

Approximate time: 5-10 minutes.

Materials: none needed; for the room version, three sheets labeled “Low”, “Middle”, “High”, laid on the floor in a line 2-3 meters apart.

  1. 1.Agree on the scale

Show the scale with your hand: hand down is “low”, at chest level is “middle”, raised is “high”. Agree together what it means for this group: energy, willingness to work, mood in general. One scale per meeting.

  1. 2.Mark the mood

On the count of “three”, everyone shows their position with their hand at the same time. Movement version: participants stand by the sheet that fits. Showing at the same time helps people not adjust to others.

  1. 3.Comment briefly

Anyone who wishes says in one sentence what influenced their mark. Explaining is optional. The leader sums up without judging: “Today our moods vary, from low to high.”

  1. 4.Use the result

If many participants showed “low”, offer a calmer start or a short break. Repeat the barometer at the end to see the change.

With children aged 7-10

Replace the words with weather pictures: a cloud, the sun behind a cloud, the sun. A child can simply point to a picture.

If someone mocks

Stop comments like “He’s at the bottom again” at once: “Every mark is fine here; we don’t comment on them.” If a participant regularly shows a very low state, talk with them separately after the meeting, not in front of the group.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widespread group-work format; the author is unknown. Instructions written by the Metafora editors.
