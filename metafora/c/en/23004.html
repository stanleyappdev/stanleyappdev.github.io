Art technique

The power of a name

  • Children's self-esteem
  • Self-acceptance
  • Self-confidence
  • Who am I?
  • Resources

Goal: support a good image of oneself through qualities and skills that the child or teenager names themselves.

Approximate time: 15-25 minutes.

Materials: a sheet of paper, markers, colored pencils.

  1. 1.Write the name

The name is written in a column, each letter on a new line.

  1. 2.Choose words

For each letter, choose a word about yourself: a quality, a skill, a hobby. For example, “Oleh”: O - orderly, L - loves football, E - energetic, H - hospitable.

  1. 3.Decorate

The letters can be colored, outlined with patterns, and drawings added.

  1. 4.Discuss
  • ✓Which word do you like most?
  • ✓When did one of these traits come in handy recently?
  • ✓Who else notices this trait in you?
  1. 5.Close

Suggest hanging the sheet where it can be seen, or photographing it.

With teenagers

A pseudonym or nickname can be used instead of the name. The words can be more serious: values, principles, what others appreciate in you.

Note for the practitioner

If it is hard to find a word for a letter, a word with that letter inside it, or a drawing instead of a word, will do. The adult can suggest, but the child decides.
