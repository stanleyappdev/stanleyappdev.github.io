Game

Introduce yourself in character

  • Self-presentation
  • Expressing emotions
  • Imagination
  • Communication
  • Nonverbal communication

Goal: get acquainted and play with voice, posture and gesture.

Approximate time: 15-20 minutes.

Materials: none needed.

  1. 1.Introducing yourself in character

Participants sit in a circle. The leader suggests a mood or character: sleepy, proud, shy, hurried, surprised. One by one, each person stands up and says their name the way such a character would: with a matching voice, posture and gesture. After a round the mood changes.

  1. 2.Riddle variant

A participant chooses a fairy-tale hero everyone knows and introduces themselves as that hero with one phrase or gesture, without naming them. The others guess.

  1. 3.Leaving the role

After their turn, the participant “takes off the role”: shakes out their hands and says their name in their ordinary voice. Anyone who does not want to stand up can introduce themselves sitting down.

  1. 4.Discuss
  • ✓What was it like to introduce yourself as someone else?
  • ✓What changed the impression most: voice, movement or facial expression?
  1. 5.Close

A last round: everyone says their name in their ordinary voice and one word about their mood right now.

With children aged 7-10

The leader shows each character first, and children can copy.

With teenagers aged 11-14

Let participants suggest characters themselves.

If someone mocks

Characters are not used to imitate anyone present. If this happens, stop the game and restate the rule; invite the person affected to choose the next character.

SOURCE AND ORIGIN

A widespread group game; the author is unknown. Instructions written by the Metafora editors.
