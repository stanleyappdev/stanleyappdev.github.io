Exercise

Kind words mailbox

  • Friendship
  • Bullying
  • Communication
  • Support
  • Active listening
  • Feedback

Goal: over a week, notice classmates’ kind actions and write about them so that every child receives support.

Approximate time: 15 minutes to launch, 5 minutes each day, 20 minutes for handing out the mail and discussing it.

Materials: a box with a slot (the “mailbox”), identical cards, envelopes or labeled pockets for each child, a class list for the leader.

  1. 1.Launch the mail

Explain the rules:

  • ✓each card is signed: from whom and to whom;
  • ✓we write about a specific action or quality: “Thank you for sharing your paints”, “I like the way you explain problems”;
  • ✓no jokes about looks and no anonymous notes;
  • ✓everyone writes to at least two different people during the week.
  1. 2.Make sure nobody is left without mail

The leader uses the list to keep track of who has already received cards. Each day you can draw the name of a “recipient of the day” from the box, to whom anyone who wants writes, or assign each participant a person they will definitely write to.

  1. 3.Check the mail

The leader reads the cards before handing them out. Cards with mockery or double meanings are not handed out; the leader talks with the author separately. Without checking, this format easily becomes a tool for bullying.

  1. 4.Hand out the mail

At the end of the week the cards go into the pockets. Everyone reads their own; reading aloud is not required.

  1. 5.Discuss
  • ✓What was it like to receive mail?
  • ✓What was easier: writing or reading?
  • ✓What kind action did you notice this week?
  1. 6.Close

The cards stay with their recipients. Invite everyone to write one more thank-you outside the mail to anyone they choose.

Note for the practitioner

If a child received noticeably fewer cards, don’t make this visible to the group; next time strengthen the way recipients are assigned.
